# MU GRAD Exam Prep Kit

A self-study kit for the **MU GRAD TEST / MU GRAD PLUS** — the English
proficiency examination administered by the Language Center, Faculty of
Graduate Studies, **Mahidol University**, used for graduate admission and
graduation English requirements.

> ⚠️ **Always verify current rules before you register.** Test format, fees,

## Test at a glance

**MU GRAD TEST (Computer-based)** — 100 points total, ~3 hours:

| Section | Points | Time | Content |
|---------|--------|------|---------|
| Listening | 20 | ~30 min | 4 passages × 5 questions; each passage played twice |
| Structure (Grammar) | 20 | ~30 min | 10 incomplete sentences + 10 written-expression (error ID) items |
| Reading | 40 | ~60 min | 5 academic passages (~500 words each), 40 questions |
| Writing | 20 | ~60 min | Essay writing task |

**MU GRAD PLUS** = the four sections above + a **Speaking section
(20 points, computer-based)**, for a total of **120 points** ("Sum of
Highest Section Scores" — your best section scores across test dates are
combined, so retakes can target specific sections). Many programs require
MU GRAD PLUS with minimum sub-scores in Writing and Speaking — check your
program's announcement.

## Score requirements (verify for your program!)

Per the official table for student IDs **63XXXXX onwards**, the relevant
benchmark here is **MU GRAD Plus 70, with Writing ≥ 10 and Speaking ≥ 10**
(the table's tiers: 40 minimum / 70 with W=10, S=10 / 90 with W=12, S=12;
TOEFL-iBT and MU ELT equivalents also accepted). Requirements differ by
degree level, program, and purpose — re-check your faculty's current
announcement at <https://graduate.mahidol.ac.th/> before each registration.

Alternative accepted tests usually include IELTS, TOEFL iBT — if you already
hold a valid score, check the equivalency table before registering.

## How to use this kit

1. Read [section-strategies.md](section-strategies.md) once, end to end.
2. Take a timed diagnostic (see [resources.md](resources.md)) — or use your
   latest official score report — to find your weakest sections.
3. Follow [study-plan.md](study-plan.md), weighting time toward your gaps.
   Sections below their sub-score floors (Writing/Speaking ≥ 10) come
   first: no total passes without them. After that, Grammar responds
   fastest to drilling, and Reading holds the most points (40).
4. Drill [vocabulary.md](vocabulary.md) daily (15–20 min).
5. In the final week, do full timed simulations only.

**Superscoring warning:** the portal's "My Best Scores" combines your
highest section scores across test dates, so retakes can target specific
sections — but every counting section must still be inside its **2-year
validity window** at the same time. Don't let banked sections expire
before you complete the pass; plan retakes against the earliest expiry
date among the scores you're counting on.
