# 8-Week Study Plan (MU GRAD TEST / MU GRAD PLUS)

Assumes ~1–1.5 hours on weekdays and ~2–3 hours on one weekend day.
If you only have **4 weeks**, do two weeks of this plan per week and cut the
"light" days, keeping every timed simulation.

> **Weighting tip:** the passing benchmark is **70/120 with Writing ≥ 10
> and Speaking ≥ 10**. If either of those sections is below its floor, do
> the Week 6 Speaking drills **daily from Week 1** (10 min/day) and add one
> timed essay per week from Week 2 — no total passes without the floors.
> Grammar and Reading then supply the bulk of the remaining total. If you
> have a recent official score report, it can replace Week 1's diagnostic.

Daily constants (every day, all 8 weeks):

- **15–20 min vocabulary** — flashcards from [vocabulary.md](vocabulary.md)
- **10 min English listening** — a podcast, lecture clip, or news segment

---

## Week 1 — Diagnose and set a baseline

- Take one full timed practice test (all sections, strict timing).
- Score it by section and convert to the 20/20/40/20 weighting.
- Write down: target score (from your program's announcement), current
  score, and the two weakest sections.
- Read all of [section-strategies.md](section-strategies.md).

## Week 2 — Grammar foundation

- Review the 12 high-yield grammar points in
  [section-strategies.md](section-strategies.md#structure-grammar--20-points).
- Do 20 incomplete-sentence items + 20 error-identification items per day
  (TOEFL ITP "Structure & Written Expression" materials work well — the
  format is nearly identical).
- Keep an **error log**: every miss, one line — the rule you broke and the fix.

## Week 3 — Reading I (comprehension skills)

- One ~500-word academic passage per day, untimed but focused:
  main idea, detail, inference, vocabulary-in-context questions.
- Practice paragraph-level skimming: 30 seconds per paragraph, then write a
  5-word summary of each.
- Sources: TOEFL/IELTS academic reading practice, Scientific American,
  The Conversation.

## Week 4 — Reading II (speed) + Listening

- Timed reading: 5 passages in 60 minutes (12 min per passage). Twice this week.
- Listening daily: one 3–5 minute academic clip, listened to **twice**
  (matching the exam's replay rule), then answer or write a summary.
- Note-taking drill: numbers, names, cause→effect links.

## Week 5 — Writing

- Learn the 4-paragraph template in
  [section-strategies.md](section-strategies.md#writing--20-points).
- Write 3 timed essays this week (60 min each, typed — the exam is
  computer-based, so practice typing).
- After each essay: self-check with the checklist, then rewrite the worst
  paragraph.

## Week 6 — Speaking (MU GRAD PLUS) or Reading III

**If taking MU GRAD PLUS:**
- Daily 1–2 minute recorded responses to prompts (describe, compare, give an
  opinion). Speaking to a computer feels awkward — practice exactly that.
- Listen back and fix ONE thing per recording (pace, fillers, verb tense).

**If taking MU GRAD TEST only:** repeat Week 4's timed reading routine.

## Week 7 — Full simulations

- Two full timed tests this week, morning schedule, no pauses.
- Between them: review only your error log and rewrite one essay.

## Week 8 — Taper and logistics

- One final simulation early in the week; light review after.
- Re-read your error log daily — it is now your highest-yield document.
- Logistics: confirm registration, test center/computer requirements, ID,
  arrival time. Sleep normally the last two nights.
